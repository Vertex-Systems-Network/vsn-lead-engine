# Internal normalized billing evidence and reconciliation contract

This slice implements a pure server-only validator, not payment processing or entitlement reconciliation. `core.billing_evidence.verified_billing_event` accepts one exact-byte signed normalized snapshot and trusted server binding coordinates. It has no database access, endpoint, provider client, queue, subscription checkout or entitlement/counter write. `SAAS_BILLING_VERIFIERS` defaults to an empty map and cannot be populated by event bodies, HTTP or frontend/environment input. Synthetic test keys do not represent a provider account or commercial rights.

## Version 1 evidence boundary

A trusted future adapter must first verify its actual provider's webhook signature/account/event semantics, independently determine authoritative subscription state and map it to an enabled local binding. Only then may it produce this internal HMAC-SHA256 snapshot. This normalized signature is not a native provider webhook signature and does not itself prove money received, a purchased plan, commercial terms or an authentic provider. Provisioning the issuer/key or enabling a binding remains a separately reviewed deployment/provider capability.

The body is UTF-8 JSON, at most 8 KiB, with exactly `version`, `key_id`, `event_ref`, `issuer_code`, `binding_id`, `workspace_id`, `revision`, `issued_at`, `valid_until`, `active` and `limits`. Duplicate names at any nesting level, missing/extra fields, non-object/recursive/unrepresentable data, boolean/float integer substitutions and malformed coordinates fail with the same generic error. HMAC covers the original bytes; reserialization or whitespace changes require a different signature. The registry is keyed by the trusted binding's issuer then bounded key ID; at least 32 secret bytes are required. Revoked/unknown/malformed verifier configuration fails closed.

Binding/workspace are canonical local UUID strings matching trusted `BillingBinding` coordinates. A caller must never construct that binding from untrusted event claims. Issuer/key IDs use bounded ASCII identifiers (64 characters), event reference at most 96. Revision is an integer 1–2,147,483,647. Issuance is an integer UTC Unix timestamp within the preceding 24 hours or at most five minutes ahead. Active snapshots have a future integer `valid_until` after both issuance and current time, at most 366 days after issuance. Inactive snapshots have `valid_until == issued_at` and zero limits. Limits contain exactly nonnegative bounded integer `leads`, `jobs`, `provider_calls` and `exports`. They are complete capabilities, not prices, credits, purchased quantities, provider permission or usage-reset instructions.

The return value is immutable typed evidence with event/key references, exact body hash, revision, issuance/validity, active flag and four limits. It retains no body, signature, secret or raw payment/customer/account/subscription data and suppresses content-bearing default representations. Nothing is persisted/logged by the validator; references/hash are still pseudonymous and must not be treated as anonymous or logged by a future caller. Verification alone does not enforce event uniqueness, ordering, current actor role, durable binding enablement or ongoing entitlement expiry.
